# Al-Fath Berkarya — Product Vision ("What I Want")

*The full vision, articulated comprehensively — the dream in its complete form, broader than what gets built first. For the buildable, prioritized subset, see the PRD; this document is the north star it serves. (Codename: BuildersNetwork = the engine · Al-Fath Berkarya = the programme.)*

---

## The Heart

Al-Fath Berkarya is a community platform built around the **karya** — the work itself — where members find their direction, build in the open, raise each other's collective knowledge, and find the people to build with. Every feature below exists to make **collaboration easy, knowledge shared, and inspiration constant** — and to do it as a *productive* space, not an attention trap.

---

## 1. Projects as Living, Collaborative Hubs

A project (karya) isn't a static listing — it's a living hub designed to pull people in and make contributing effortless. Each project page should have:

- **Contributors shown as people** — profile pictures of everyone who's contributed, so a project has visible faces, not just a title.
- **Project posts** — a stream of updates covering **progress, challenges, and achievements**, so anyone can follow the journey and feel its momentum.
- **A per-project AI** — an assistant scoped to *that* project that anyone can ask, for example:
  - *"Ini dibuat pakai apa?"* — "What's this built with?"
  - *"Gimana caranya kalau aku mau kontribusi?"* — "How do I contribute?"
  - *"Apa masalah paling dekat yang project ini hadapi?"* — "What's the most pressing problem this project is facing right now?"
- **A feedback & validation channel** — community members can share constructive thoughts, reactions, and questions about the project right on its page. They aren't just spectators: a project page is where builders meet their *early users* and collect honest feedback *at every stage* — from the earliest research and conception (target-market analysis, problem–solution fit) all the way through active building. Validation comes from the community, not only from an AI.

**The intent:** drop the barrier to collaboration to near-zero. A curious member can understand a project and find their way in without interrupting anyone or reading a wall of docs. Projects become open doors, not closed shops.

---

## 2. Projects as Community Efforts / Incubation

Some projects are meant to be **collective efforts** the whole community rallies behind — for example, an existing edu / tech-for-good project that grows into something the community builds together, especially members who can code or are learning to. The platform should make it natural for a project to invite the community in and become a shared, "incubated" effort.

---

## 3. The Problem Bank

A bank of real-world problems worth solving — so when there's a stubborn problem out there, the community can rally and **hajar bareng-bareng** (tackle it together). Projects can form around these problems; the bank is where "what's worth doing" lives.

---

## 4. Collective Knowledge (three layers)

Raising the community's shared knowledge is a first-class goal, delivered in three complementary formats:

- **Microblogging feed** — short-form, like X or Threads, *but with no brainrot.* Only beneficial, worth-your-time content from people you actually know — progress, learnings, and thoughts that make people better.
- **Blog** — longer-form how-tos and guides that level up the community, e.g. *"how to code comfortably with Claude."* Durable knowledge, not just a passing post.
- **Magazine** *(aspirational)* — a periodic, curated *Al-Fath Berkarya* magazine: the editorial highlight reel of the community's best work and knowledge.

---

## 5. The AI-Guided Build Journey ("Supercharged Ideation Platform")

The platform shouldn't just *host* projects — it should actively **help them get better.** When a member starts a project, the platform guides them on a tour through the stages of building, with AI support at each step:

- product validation,
- target market analysis,
- problem–solution fit,
- UI/UX prototyping,
- and other support along the way.

The dream: building *on* Al-Fath Berkarya makes you a better builder. The platform is a mentor, not just a wall to pin your work on.

---

## 6. The Al-Fath Berkarya Badge

An embeddable **badge** that member and incubated projects can place in their footers — *"Built at / Incubated by Al-Fath Berkarya."* It spreads the community's identity outward, signals belonging, and gives projects a mark of pride — the way projects wear a Product Hunt or accelerator badge.

---

## 7. AI Everywhere — and How It's Paid For

AI is woven throughout: the onboarding/creation agent, the per-project assistant, and the guided build journey. This is intentional — AI is part of the magic, not a cost to minimize into a corner. It stays sustainable through:

- **Quota / rationing for free members** — everyone gets a fair, metered amount of AI; not unlimited.
- **A subscription tier** for genuinely impressive premium features, if and when those exist.

This both controls cost *and* opens a real revenue path — the thing Sage never had. (This is a deliberate evolution of the earlier "AI only at creation" stance: AI can be pervasive now, because quota + subscription make it sustainable.)

---

## 8. The Spirit (non-negotiables)

Whatever gets built holds to:

- **Productive, not addictive.** No brainrot, no dark patterns — the feed makes you better, not hooked.
- **Karya-centric.** The work is the unit; *any idea goes.*
- **Community-scoped & authentic.** Built for Al-Fath, in the community's real voice — not a translated Silicon Valley costume.
- **Edit-first AI.** Anything the AI generates is an editable draft, never a locked black box.
- **Desktop-first.** The build work happens at a desk; mobile stays supported for consumption, but desktop is the design target.

---

## Now / Next / Horizon

A rough sense of sequence, so the vision reads as priorities rather than a firehose:

- **Foundation (now):** project/karya hubs (contributors + project posts), profiles + onboarding agent, the problem bank, the microblogging feed, matchmaking, messaging.
- **Next:** the per-project AI assistant, the blog, the Al-Fath Berkarya badge.
- **Horizon:** the full AI-guided build journey, the magazine, subscription tiers and premium AI features.

*(The PRD's P0/P1/P2 is the engineering-grade version of this sequencing; this is the vision-grade one.)*
