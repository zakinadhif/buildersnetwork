## Why

## Scope sketch

- Recruit ~20–50 already-building members; seed real karya + real progress posts.
- Team acts as relentless first commenter.
- NFR pass: desktop-first polish (NFR-1), mobile-responsive consumption, performance (NFR-5), no-data-loss on edits (NFR-7), privacy (NFR-4 — DMs private, minors-appropriate surfaces).
- Indonesian voice validation by community members (NFR-3, AI-5).

## Exit

Launch with a populated feed: real members, real karya, real progress posts, NFRs verified.
